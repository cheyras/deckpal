# Landing photographs: provenance

Every photograph in this directory except `github.*` is a **generated scene with real card
art composited in**. None of them is a photograph of a real person, place or event.

- **Scenes:** Gemini 3 Pro Image (`google/gemini-3-pro-image`) through the Vercel AI
  Gateway, generated by `scripts/gen-landing-photos.mjs` (each shot's prompt is in its
  `SHOTS` table). Every readable card was generated as a blank white face; the prompts
  forbid text, logos and printed card designs.
  - **Round 1, 2026-10-03:** eight "league night" game-store shots, 35 images generated
    (32 Gemini, 2 `openai/gpt-image-2`, 1 `bfl/flux-2-max`). Only `hero` and `closing`
    still ship; round 2 replaced the other six.
  - **Round 2, 2026-10-04:** five panel backdrops, each in a different place, light and
    camera angle: `plan` (top-down flat lay of a binder on a desk in daylight, 4:3),
    `playtest` (a home desk at night under a lamp, 3/4 overhead, 4:3), `tune` (a deck laid
    out on a playmat by a window, high angle, 4:3), `build` (singles in toploaders spilling
    from a mailer on a kitchen counter, 4:3) and `ask` (a first-person hand of seven on
    the couch in lamp light, 3:2). 15 Gemini images were generated to reach five picks.
- **Cards:** real card art from DeckPal's own image store
  (`https://deckpal.app/deckpal/images/en/<serie>/<set>/<localId>/high.webp`), used
  nominatively like everywhere else in the app (see the site footer). Composited by
  `tools/landing-photos/composite.py`, which uses each blank face as its light map so the
  art takes the scene's perspective, shading, colour, focus and grain. No card text on the
  page is model-generated. The round-2 cards come from the sample deck in
  `apps/web/src/routes/landing/data.ts` (the `plan` binder holds Pitch Black 028-045 in
  collector order; `build` holds the sample deck's missing singles).
- **Retouching:** generated pseudo-text marks in round 1 were painted out by the same tool.
  Round 2 needed none.
- **`github.*`:** a real screenshot of the public repository page
  (`github.com/cheyras/deckpal`), cropped to the repo name, tabs, commit count and two
  folders, with the notification button masked out. Not generated.
- **Reproducing:** `tools/landing-photos/specs/<shot>.json` freezes every card's position;
  `node tools/landing-photos/fetch-art.mjs` downloads the art they name, `composite.py
  compose specs/<shot>.json` rebuilds a final, and `node scripts/gen-landing-photos.mjs
  encode [--only a,b]` writes the AVIF/WebP widths here plus `MANIFEST.json` (measured
  from disk). Raw candidates and finals live in the gitignored `.marketing-raw/landing/`.
