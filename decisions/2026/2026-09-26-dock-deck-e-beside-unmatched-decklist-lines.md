## 2026-09-26 — Dock Deck-E beside unmatched decklist lines
**Decided by:** Chey (via Claude)
**Decision:** Keep one unmatched-lines group throughout deck import. Deck-E offers help from a small dock at that group's edge; his suggestions replace the original text within each affected row, with the original struck through, a reason and Undo. Unfixed rows retain Edit. When the character is hidden, a quiet header action offers the same fix request. The primary action says "Import without them" while lines remain unresolved and "Import deck" when all are fixed.
**Why:** Chey found the separate review card and prominent Ask Deck-E button detached from the lines needing attention. Keeping the offer and each result beside its source makes the change easier to scan and judge.
**Implications:** A partial set of accepted fixes is dry-run checked before the explicit skip action imports it. If that check finds any newly unmatched line, the dialog shows it instead of skipping it without consent. The Deck-E request remains read-only; Undo, manual Edit, entitlement and hidden-character behavior remain available.
