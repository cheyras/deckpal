## 2026-11-17 — Deck-E Quick consults on close games
**Decided by:** Chey (via Claude)

**Decision:** On the Quick tier, on a `battle_log` or `battle_review` route, a `battle_digest` result for a genuinely close game ends with one conditional line addressed to the model: if you are about to write this game's review and you hold `consult`, call it once first, with the parts of the digest that matter (under `CONSULT_BRIEF_MAX`, 8,000 characters) and the reader's own words as the brief and "what decided this game, and the one lesson" as the question; if you are only answering a question about the game, or do not hold `consult`, ignore this. The shared tool's text is unchanged; its `structured` echo (`digestEcho` in `packages/agent-tools`) carries the facts — `closeGame`, `endReason`, `ownerKnown`, `finalPrizes`, `lateLeadChange`, `reviewDepth`. Deck-E's adapter appends the line (`apps/api/src/decke/closeGameConsult.ts`) only when `api/chat.mjs` passes `consultOffered` from `closeGameConsultOffered(tierOffersConsult(tier), pathways)` — the predicate `toolsForTier` uses, plus the routed pathway.

"Genuinely close" is narrower than the digest's own `closeGame` (which stays as it is: it drives the "Close game" badge and counts any lead change): the game did not end in a concession, the owner is known, and either the final gap was at most 2 Prizes with someone at 3 or more, or the lead changed hands after both sides had taken 3. A close game whose depth line says brief still gets the consult, and its review stays brief (the **Read** line, from the consult's answer); the battle_log pathway, the note and the `consult` tool description all say so. Any other brief game gets no consult.

**Why:** The live replay probe `battle-log-digest-consult` (a close 4–6 loss on Quick) read the digest and skipped `consult` in 2 of 2 runs, so Haiku wrote the review himself. The pathway is read once, long before the digest arrives; the digest result is the last thing he reads before writing. The other options were a firmer pathway line alone (kept as well, but a prompt line is what already failed) or routing close games to Standard (triage cannot see closeness before the game is logged).

The trigger was narrowed on review (2026-11-25) because a consult costs the reader credits (a Sonnet call metered at cost, up to `CONSULT_MAX_PER_REQUEST` = 2 per request) and the first version fired on cases that do not deserve one: the digest's `closeGame` calls a 1–6 loss "close" when the reader took the first Prize (the lead changed hands once), and a concession at 1–2 passes the gap test. An ownerless digest has no side to review. And the note rode on every Quick `battle_digest` call — a lookup ("what turn did I take my first Prize in game 7?") or a comparison of several games would have spent up to two consults on games nobody asked to have reviewed. Gating on the routed pathway and making the wording conditional keeps the spend on the one case the probe measured: writing the review of a game that was actually close.

**Implications:** MCP output is unchanged: `toCallToolResult` never sends `structured` (SPEC entry 17a says so), and tests in `packages/agent-tools`, `apps/api` and `apps/mcp` pin that the shared text never names `consult`. The consult's limits are unchanged (at most 2 per request, metered at cost, answer never grounding). The note is part of the tool output the browser replays for `FULL_REPLAY_TURNS` later turns, which may be on Standard; the conditional wording is what keeps it from naming a tool those turns lack, and `closeGameConsult.test.ts` pins that wording. To change which games qualify, edit `isConsultWorthyClose`; to change who gets the note, `closeGameConsultOffered`. The replay probe (`scripts/decke-replay-probe.mjs`) renders `battle_digest` from its own fixture (`renderBattleDigest`), not through Deck-E's adapter, so as it stands it measures the pathway line only; to confirm the note moved the behavior, teach its fixture to append `closeGameConsultNote` for the request's tier and pathways, then run `node scripts/decke-replay-probe.mjs --arms routed --n 3 --scenarios battle-log-digest-consult --budget-usd 1`.
