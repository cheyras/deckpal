## 2026-09-26 — "Show me" is one planned motion: one scroll owner, and a tool answers when he lands
**Decided by:** Chey (via Claude)
**Decision:** Deck-E taking the reader to something is one choreography with one owner of each moving part. (1) Only his flight scrolls the page on his behalf (`flyTo` with `scrollWith`, on the flight's clock). A page asked to `decke:reveal` a card answers by MOUNTING its row (`GridView`'s `rangeExtractor`), never by scrolling to it; `SetDetail` answers a card it can never show with an immediate `decke:reveal-miss`. (2) A driven flight aims at where the target WILL be when the scroll lands (`DeckE.planned`), and a queued via-background leg is re-solved when it launches (`viaLeg`); a presentation that rests on the far plane flies there in one leg. (3) Long pages are thrown to within `MAX_GLIDE_SCREENS` (1.5) of the target and the rest is glided with him — thrown before the new page first paints after a navigation. (4) Nothing moves him but a flight: `DeckE.hold()` pins him to the spot he occupies before the chat minimises, before a `goTo` navigates, and before a press that may navigate. (5) A driven scroll ends on the reader's real input (wheel, touch, scroll keys), never on `scrollY` disagreeing with the last write; a target the reader scrolls out of view is not chased, and the tool says he stopped short. (6) `flyTo` and a `goTo` with a selector answer from the engine's arrival callback, bounded by how long he is actually in the air rather than by a wall-clock cap. Also fixed on the way: journeys dropped `point` (sent as `then`), `ensure` waited only for the opener, a walk counted typing in the chat as "you took over", the speech bubble never avoided the ringed card (it was handed an `id` card tiles do not have), `screenRect` ignored the in-flight page shift, `returnHome` left a queued leg to fire, a stale 250 ms document-height cache read a navigation's scroll as an 18,350 px rubber-band, and the ring now settles in and fades out. (7) His speech bubble rides him: it follows `DeckE.onFrame` (the frame he is drawn in, written as a transform, no React render) instead of a position polled at 8 Hz; its side is chosen only at beats — when it appears and when he lands, or when it would cover him or the ringed card — for the line it will hold, preferring the nearest clear slot, and a change of side is eased on his clock; new words grow it away from him from the edge that faces him; it shows only his current line (`currentLine`: the words since his last action), with typing dots in place while the next line is coming. Caption slots above and below the ringed card keep it beside him on a phone. (8) The router does not reset scroll for a navigation that shows something (`resetScroll: false`); his first scroll is the page's first scroll. A Stop settles an arrival wait at once, a driven scroll also ends when the scroll lands somewhere the drive never wrote for two frames (the scrollbar), and scroll keys pressed in a field or on a control do not count as the reader taking over.
**Why:** Measured on "show me my Charizard" (card 199 of 250, another route), headless Chromium at 1440x900 with a GPU, back to back under the machine lock: ask-to-ring 4.7 s → 1.9 s; page scrolls 2 glides → 1 jump + 1 glide; single-frame snaps 4 (worst 668 px) → 0 (worst 16 px); landing 460 px from the card → 14 px; the model's "There it is" streamed mid-flight → after the ring. Phone 4.1 → 2.0 s; WebKit 3.6 → 1.9 s; iOS Safari (simulator) 4.2 → 3.2 s, where before the page stopped short because Safari reads `scrollY` back a frame late and the drive took that for the reader. The bubble moved against him by up to 668 px in a single frame (desktop) and 405 px (phone WebKit) and appended the arrival line to the departure line; now at most 2–18 px outside its one eased change of side, on desktop, phone, WebKit and iOS Safari. Under 4x CPU throttling at phone size, dropped frames during the trip went 41–44 → 9. Earlier fixes did not stick because each one added a watcher or a wait for one symptom (the grid's smooth scroll was made to take turns with the flight's instead of being removed; a fixed 1.1 s beat stood in for "he has landed") and none was ever measured again: the only CI check of Deck-E asserted where he stood, never how he moved.
**Implications:** `tests/browser/deckeShow.mjs` now advances the real engine 1/60 s per frame without drawing it (`DeckE.simulate`) in Chromium and WebKit, desktop and 390 px, and fails on a second scroll, a snap over 90 px, a landing over 40 px from the card, an answer sent before the ring, a bubble that moves more than 24 px against him in a frame or changes side more than twice, a slow "no" for a missing card, a chased target after a reader's scroll, or a glide under reduced motion; it runs WebKit at phone width too, and fails on the app as it was. Any new way to show something must be added to it. Anything else that rides him (a label, a pointer) must follow `onFrame`, not a poll. A page that owns a virtualized list must answer `decke:reveal` by mounting, not scrolling (the Table view in #217 must follow this). Test builds set `VITE_DECKE_TEST_HANDLE=1` to expose the engine; production never does. The rules are restated for engine readers in `apps/web/src/character/decke/README.md`, "Showing someone something: the rules".
