## 2026-11-27 — Scanner: the print picks the printing, the picture picks the card; a ladder-named row's meter is the picture's
**Decided by:** Chey (via Claude Opus 5.5)

**Decision:** Three changes, from the owner's White Flare (sv10.5w) session (SCAN-SPEC S4). No threshold moves.
- **Picture-family corroboration** (`fuse.ts` `corroborateByPicture`, used by `resolve.ts` `agreement`). Over a list a PRINTED key built (badge+number, number+denominator, name+number, name+denominator), the vector's top-1 is read as a picture: the top-1 plus its same-art siblings (`artFamilies.ts`). When exactly one printing of that picture is in the list, and the picture stands at least the model's `marginMin` clear of every card outside it, that printing is the answer (`corroborated`, `keyedBy` the rung). The print picks the printing; the picture picks the card. Name-family and family-text lists are untouched, because a name cannot rule a sibling out.
- **A misspelt evolution line is furniture** (`ocr/fields.ts` `cleanNameLine`). A run of letters within two edits of `evolvesfrom` drops the whole line, as a correctly spelt one already did. Otherwise what is left is the PRE-evolution's name, and it outnumbers the title's letters (`EvohvesfromLiiipuy` was a Herdier's name read).
- **The row's match meter shows what named the card** (`identity.ts` `rowMeter`, `FeedEntryCard`). On a row the hash named, it shows the hash's `Match N% · dist D`, as before. On a row the ladder named, it shows the picture's cosine when the vector nominated the card, and otherwise "Matched from the printed number". `ScanResolveMatch` now types the `similarity` the server already sent.
- **`scripts/scan-bench/synth.py`**: camera-like captures from high-res catalogue art, for a separate bench root. It reproduces a mechanism when no field capture is on record (README "Synthetic captures").

**Why:**
- **Energy Retrieval asked although the card prints `WHT 082/086`.** The row's hint was `read "Energy Retrieval"`, which `ocrHintLabel` shows only when no badge and no number were read. sv10.5w-082 is sv01-171's picture: the catalogue images hash to dHash 0, and the vector puts them 0.001-0.005 apart, either way round. So with no printed key the printing guard has to ask, and that part is right. The defect is in what happened when the strip WAS partly read. Replaying the ladder: `082/086` alone asked whichever twin the vector put first. When sv01-171 was first, it was outside the keyed list. When sv10.5w-082 was first, it was not "separated" from its own twin. `08/086` plus the name (a dropped digit) asked the same way.
- **"Match 81% · dist 12" on almost every row** is neither a cap nor a constant. It is the hash's bit-similarity, 1 - 12/64. On real camera captures the right card's art sits 10-13 bits away (median 12 on the 2026-11-25/26 field captures). The ladder, not the hash, names nearly every card: 223 of 231 confident bench answers. So the meter reported a demoted bystander, and it read 80-84 % whatever the card.
- **Lillipup "identified as Herdier" is not a recognition error.** Lillipup is indexed (dHash and vector gallery hold all 173 sv10.5w cards). On synthetic Lillipup captures the vector's top-1 is Lillipup at 0.90-0.95, and Herdier is not in its top 25 (below 0.44). The dHash puts Herdier 11-19 bits away, and it may name a card alone only at 7 or less. OCR reads "Lillipup", and no card prints that title but Lillipup. The Herdier row's "dist 12" means the ladder named it and the hash merely listed it, which fits a real Herdier capture. The owner then said he did have a Herdier. The same session says Lillipup "didn't scan": the "Already scanned" refusal took it (S3). See Implications. The nearest real recognition hazard runs the other way. A Stage 1 card prints its pre-evolution's name, and a misspelt "Evolves from" line slipped past the furniture filter on 2 of 276 real crops and 2 of 104 synthetic ones, handing the ladder the pre-evolution's name.
- **"pick a printing" on every row is right for this set.** Every White Flare common and uncommon has three or four standard printings (Normal, Reverse Holofoil, Poké Ball and Master Ball pattern reverses), and the scanner has no foil signal. Whether the row should ask less loudly is a review-UI question.

Bench (`scripts/scan-bench/bench.ts`):
- **Real crops, all datasets:** AUTO-ID 231 → 232 of 276, WRONG 0 → 0, NEG-FALSE 0 → 0. The new one is a Rellor sv10-024 read `024/182`, which shares its picture with sv08-013.
- **Synthetic White Flare captures** (12 session cards plus sv01-171, 8 each): AUTO-ID 94 → 95 of 104. The new one is Energy Retrieval read `08/086` plus the name. WRONG stays at 1, a hash-first answer (see Implications). The two misspelt evolution lines now read `Herdier` and `Gothorita`.
- **Leave-one-set-out** (me06 unindexed): WRONG 0.

**Implications:**
- **The Energy Retrieval capture the owner saw still asks.** Its strip produced nothing. The shipped OCR reads a number pair on about a quarter of real full-res crops (29 of 117) and the badge on fewer (20). The field strips are blurred while the white-on-black badge often stays legible. Extra passes over the same crop (tighter ROI, 4x, inverted, contrast-stretched) each added one to three right numbers and as many wrong ones, so none shipped. A badge read without a number is also not trusted yet: 3 of the 6 such reads on real crops were false (`POR` off "Pokémon ex rule"). What would fix that capture is more strip evidence, a multi-frame strip re-read in the capture code, which is the next step #398 named.
- **The hash can still name a wrong card first.** One synthetic Joltik (blurred, glare) hashed 5 bits from Raticate with a clear tie margin, and it settled before the vector and the name (both Joltik) arrived. Production heals this with the late correction in `Scan.tsx` (`narrowedIdentity`), after a visible swap; the bench does not model that. Holding a hash claim for the resolve leg would close it, at the cost of that leg's latency on hash-named captures. That is a capture-timing decision for the scan UI rework.
- **For S3 ("Already scanned"):** the look re-arm (`rearm.ts`, `REARM_NEW_MIN` 1.0) cannot tell White Flare cards apart. Between the session's cards (Lillipup, Herdier, Cheren, Gothorita, Galvantula, Durant, Energy Retrieval), synthetic captures measure 0.44-0.99 by `lookDistance`, all under 1.0. In a silver-frame set, every flip in place reads as the same card.
