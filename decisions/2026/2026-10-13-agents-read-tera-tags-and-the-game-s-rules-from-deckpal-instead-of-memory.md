## 2026-10-13 — Agents read Tera tags and the game's rules from DeckPal instead of memory
**Decided by:** @cheyras ("just run and do literally all of this", 2026-10-13, Phase 1 of the plan from his claude.ai skill tests), by Claude Opus 5.5

**Decision:** Three changes, all read-only.
- `get_card` prints a `tag:` line for each Tera, Ancient or Future tag a printing carries, with what the tag means in play. `search_cards` shows the tags in each row and filters with `tag`. The tags come from the battle simulator's tables, through a new data-only export, `@deckpal/sim/card-tags`.
- A new read tool, `rules_lookup` (MCP and Deck-E), returns two things:
  - DeckPal's core-rules reference, 17 entries in our own words, each pinned by a simulator test;
  - official rulings from the Pokémon Rulings Compendium's index, filed under the cards, attacks or Abilities named, with DeckPal's one-line summary where the simulator pins the ruling (433 of 1,944) and a link to the official answer. The index is `@deckpal/sim/rulings`, generated by `packages/sim/scripts/ruling-index.ts`, and a test fails when it is stale.
- Deck-E's `card_rules` and `battle_log` pathway texts send game rules to `rules_lookup` and tags to `get_card`.

**Why:** The owner tested Haiku 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5 with the DeckPal skill in claude.ai (2026-10-13). Every model, in every run, told him Nighttime Mine could not have blocked his Dragapult ex, because the card lookup showed no Tera marker. Dragapult ex is a Tera Pokémon; the game had shown him the extra cost.
- The catalog cannot say so: TCGdex has no Tera or Ancient/Future field, and `card_subtype` and `card_tag` hold no rows.
- The simulator already knew. Its `cards/tera.ts` lists both Dragapult ex printings, and a test plays exactly this case.
- Agents never saw it, because `get_card` reads only the catalog.

The same tests had models inventing rules: "a Pokémon can't attack the turn it evolves", and cutting Rare Candy slowing a Stage 1. Haiku had already scored 2/18 on a first-turn rule (2026-10-11), and that decision named a rules lookup as the real fix.

**Implications:**
- The tool surface is 29 tools (18 read). `tools/list` measures 67,639 characters against the 70,000 budget, which leaves little room for the next tools.
- The landing copy, llms.txt, the generated tool docs and the link-preview image now say 29.
- New tags or rulings arrive by regenerating the simulator's tables (`scripts/refresh-card-tables.ts`, `scripts/rulings-sync.ts`, then `pnpm --filter @deckpal/sim ruling-index`).
- No ruling text is copied. A ruling without a simulator summary shows only its link.
- The core-rules reference is read as fact. A new entry needs a simulator test or a rulebook page in `checked`.
