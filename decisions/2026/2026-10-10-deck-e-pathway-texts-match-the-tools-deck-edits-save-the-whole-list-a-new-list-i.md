## 2026-10-10 — Deck-E pathway texts match the tools: deck edits save the whole list, a new list is created before it is filled, and the numbered set says what it filters
**Decided by:** @cheyras (audit scope, what to leave alone, eval budget); built by Claude Opus 5.5 from an audit of the pathway texts against the tool code.

**Decision:** The non-battle pathway texts (`apps/api/src/decke/pathways/texts/`) now say only what the tools do. **deck_iterate** saves an agreed change with `save_deck` `mode: "edit"` and the complete list (`cards` reconciles the deck to exactly what is sent, so it is never a diff), reads the current list from `decks` with `include: ["cards"]` because the `deck_history` timeline holds no cards, builds off an older version by reading that snapshot (`version: N`), editing it and saving once instead of reverting first (`revert_to` also restores that version's strategy guide unless `include_strategy: false`), opens losses by `log_id` because list rows cut notes to 40 characters, and shows a revision as in/out lines, not `showDeck`. **deck_build** asks on one card with the four choices that matter most, skips budget and ownership for a PTCG Live deck (cards are crafted there) unless the reader raises them, walks a researched list's flex slots with the reader, offers two directions on the card and ends the turn when intake is thin, gives Pokémon as ids but lets Trainers and Energy go to `check_deck` by exact name, quotes cost only from `check_deck`, and gives the skeleton as ranges (12–20 Pokémon, 30–38 Trainers, 6–14 Energy). **collection_plan** uses DeckPal's goals (complete, master, grandmaster), looks with one `set_progress` call before asking, caps paging and `card_price_history`, and handles "the numbered set" with `rarity_exclude`, saying that it filters only the missing cards and cost. **lists** leads with `add_missing`, `mode` and `kind`, and creates a new list before filling it. **price_value** sends "what moved in my collection" to `collection_value` and drops "condition", which DeckPal does not store. The core prompt's research line adds `topic: "competitive"` for play questions, one search as the norm, and none of the reader's data in a query. Tier sentences the model cannot act on ("This is Quick work", "Move to Standard") and the nonexistent "digests" are gone from these texts.

**Why:** An audit of every pathway text against `packages/agent-tools/src/tools/*`, `decke/tools.ts` and the adapter found instructions that would misfire on real data. The worst was deck_iterate: "use `save_deck`" with no word on the list's shape invites a "-1/+1" call that deletes the other cards, and "revert to v1, then edit" took two approval cards and silently restored v1's strategy guide. `showDeck`'s Save is a PTCGL import that always creates a new deck (`apps/web/src/character/host/chat/deckSave.ts`), so a revised deck shown there and saved forks the deck and splits its battle history. Two of the audit's own claims were false in the code and were not applied: (1) "one `edit_list` call with `add_missing`" for a new list — `edit_list` refuses `add_missing` in the call that creates the list ("add_missing needs an existing list_id"; pinned by `lists-mutations.test.ts`), so the text says to create the list, then add to it by `list_id`; (2) `set_progress` with `rarity_exclude` does not filter the goal lines — the owned-of-total line still counts the whole set, secret rares included, while the missing list and cost to finish are filtered. `check_deck` was verified to resolve names (`routes/deckCheck.ts` `chooseName`: an owned printing first, then a legal one, then the newest), which is what lets Trainers and Energy go in by name. Left alone on purpose: a "preview first" line for writes (it dropped write calls to 0/15 in August; the dry-run results already say to re-call with `dry_run: false`), battle-log `player_name`, and the battle_log and battle_review texts, which get their own pass.

Measured on the replay probe, routed arm, n=2 per scenario, fixture tools, 2026-10-10, against the earlier routed run: deck-build thin/full/pushback, deck-iterate, collection-finish, price-value, card-rules, navigate, small-talk and general all 2/2 as before; collection-finish 2/2 again after its caveat was tightened (one run had told the reader the owned total counted only numbered cards); research-current-meta 3/4 (the miss is the undated answer the earlier round also recorded); lists 3/4, the miss a fixture artifact (the fixture's create returns no list id and its `lists` never shows lists written during the run, so Haiku retried the create); the new `price-value-collection-movers` 2/2 (both called `collection_value`); the new `deck-iterate-save-agreed` 0/2 on a grader false negative — `proposedChangeCount` scores a bolded `**+1 Counter Catcher.**` as zero — while its save turn was right both times (`mode: "edit"`, all 60 cards, a `version_note` citing the evidence, approved, no `showDeck`). Battle scenarios, touched only by the core research line: log-close and log-locals 2/2, log-lopsided 0/2 (one note left out of `notes`, one reply over 800 characters; the earlier round was 1/2 on the same length check), battle-review 0/2 as before (the same bold-markdown grader miss). Live spend $1.006.

**Implications:**
- `showDeck` is avoided for revisions of an existing deck until the widget can save a new version of it; the core prompt and the `showDeck` tool description still say "proposed or revised deck" and should change with that widget work.
- The core prompt is 15,986 characters against the 16,000 budget `prompt.test.ts` enforces; the next core addition needs a cut.
- `pathways.test.ts` pins the new facts (whole-list edit saves, no revert-first, goal names, create-then-add, no "condition", no tier sentences, no "digest", no Deep Think) and exempts battle_log and battle_review from the tier/digest check until their pass; take them out of `OWN_PASS` then.
- Probe fixes owed (not prompt changes): `proposedChangeCount` should strip markdown emphasis; the fixture's `edit_list` should refuse `add_missing` on create and return a list id, and its `lists` should show lists written during a run; `world.json` Trainer ids such as `trainer-nest-ball` fail grounding's card-id shape, so every probe `showDeck` refuses ("50/60").
- Two scenarios were added to `scripts/fixtures/decke-replay/scenarios.json`: `deck-iterate-save-agreed` and `price-value-collection-movers`.
- MCP clients do not get these texts; if they ever do (PLAN §4.5), the writing guidance needs a variant for callers without Deck-E's approval hold.
