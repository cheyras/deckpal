## 2026-10-10 — Deep Think: Deck-E offers Claude Opus 5.5 with a cost and runs it only on the reader's signed OK
**Decided by:** @cheyras (the name, "always ask, with a cost"); built by Claude Opus 5.5 directing Codex workers and Claude subagents.

**Decision:** Deck-E's top tier is **Deep Think**, Claude Opus 5.5 at effort `high`. It exists only where approvals are signed (`DECKE_APPROVAL_SECRET`) and credits are metered — a paid wallet (priced) or an unlimited account (no price line); the daily allowance, credits off and flat v1 pricing never see it. Deck-E offers it with a `deep_think` tool (`why`, `plan`) only when the request truly benefits — a game with a lot to learn from, a season of results, tournament prep with a meta read — or when the reader asks for depth (triage `wantsDeep`). The tool needs approval, so the AI SDK raises the signed consent card; beside it the server streams an offer (`data-decke-deep-offer`): a token binding that card to this turn and, for a paid wallet, a server-computed estimate ("About 40–120 credits", `estimateCredits` per pathway) shown above the model-written reason and plan, never a model-written number. "Use Deep Think" waits for the balance read after the card went up and becomes Top up below the estimate's high end.

A request reaches Opus by two routes only (`decke/deepThink.ts`): **the approval leg**, whose final message ENDS with an approved `deep_think` answer — exactly the shape ai@7 validates by HMAC before any model call — that also carries this turn's offer token (the SDK's HMAC covers the call, not the turn); and **a later leg of the same turn** carrying the grant `deep_think`'s execute mints only after the SDK accepted the signature (HMAC over user, conversation, exchange and the reader's message, 15 minutes). Both outrank the browser's route echo, and only they add the hold multiplier: migration 083 gives `decke_metered_begin` a multiplier bounded 1–10 (Deep asks for 8× the 25-credit hold); settlement is still the Gateway-reported cost and the unused hold returns. A paid reader below the estimate's HIGH end answers on Standard with a one-line note. "Keep it quick" sends its own reason and the turn answers on Standard; once a turn has a Deep Think answer the offer leaves view for it, and a Deep run cannot ask the reader questions (it states assumptions).

**Why:** The owner wants the expensive reasoning available "when it truly, truly would benefit", asked for with a name and a cost rather than prompted for constantly (2026-10-10). Opus 5.5 costs $4 / $20 per MTok — twice Sonnet 5.5 — and a deep analysis with research can run well past the ordinary 25-credit hold, which would stop the chat mid-work (`meteredCapReached`). Consent is a signed approval because that is the one mechanism in Deck-E the browser cannot forge — but only where the SDK actually checks it. Review (2026-10-10) reproduced Opus calls from an approved part the SDK never validates (an `output-available` part, or a yes in an earlier message), from any part with signing off, and from a genuine approval replayed into a later turn; the routes above close each, and `deepThink.test.ts` / `deepThinkSdk.test.ts` pin them against the real SDK. The floor is the high end because admission needs only 3 credits, the hold is `least(200, balance)` and the cap is checked only when a model operation starts, so one 32k-token Opus step could otherwise overshoot a small hold into the overage buffer, repeatably.

**Implications:**
- Migration 083 must be applied to production before this deploys; its function body is 081's byte-for-byte except the bounded multiplier, and its grants restore 081's exactly (the CI DB suite, `metered.mjs`, covers default, 8× and out-of-range).
- No new environment variable: the tokens are keyed by `DECKE_APPROVAL_SECRET`, and with it unset Deep Think is off (the boot warning says so).
- Approval is per turn; the next reader message starts on whatever triage picks.
- Browser proof: `tests/browser/deepThinkCard.mjs` at 1440 and 390 (docked card, price above the prose, disabled until the balance is known, decline reason, offer token on the answer, the grant replayed on the leg after the approval with no unpaired call, the "Used Deep Think" row).
