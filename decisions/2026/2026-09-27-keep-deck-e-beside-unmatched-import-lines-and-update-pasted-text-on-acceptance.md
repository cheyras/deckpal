## 2026-09-27 — Keep Deck-E beside unmatched import lines and update pasted text on acceptance
**Decided by:** Chey (via Codex)

**Decision:** Keep Deck-E parked beside unmatched import lines. Accepting a suggested correction replaces that physical line in the paste box; Undo restores its original text. Show each correction and its Undo in its own review row, even if another edit creates a duplicate unmatched line.

**Why:** Chey chose the parked presentation and an editable paste box that reflects the corrections the reader accepted. A duplicate line previously consumed the review list's text count and hid a retained correction and its Undo.

**Implications:** Suggestions remain tied to physical line identities while unrelated lines change. The paste box is the text checked and imported. Review rows include retained corrections independently of unmatched-text counts, and Undo changes only its own line.
