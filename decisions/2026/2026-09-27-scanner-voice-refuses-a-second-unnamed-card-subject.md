## 2026-09-27 — Scanner voice refuses a second unnamed card subject
**Decided by:** Chey (via Codex gpt-6-sol)

**Decision:** Refuse a new card reference after a clause break when a printing was already requested, even if neither clause names a card.

**Why:** “This one’s a holo and that one’s reverse” can refer to two captures. Applying the second printing to the first clause’s captured card would silently edit the wrong card.

**Implications:** The reader repeats the second clause as a separate command. Self-corrections without a fresh subject, such as “holo, no, reverse,” still work. This extends the existing one-command, one-capture rule without a schema or deployment change.
