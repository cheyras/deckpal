## Usage claim (per the video)

Introduced in the Platinum series as a denser, more silvery redesign of Cosmos; became THE default promo pattern (tins, blisters, order bonuses). SV era also uses cosmos borders on regular cards.

## Notes

aka 'pixel cosmos' / 'retro cosmos'. Less empty space than WOTC cosmos; vintage foil reads deeper/more mirror-like. The interlude's Cleffa is actually Cosmos II.
